PRODUCT / FEATURES

What CS Moments can do.

CS Moments is a Windows desktop application that helps turn video recordings into short vertical edits. You choose the source, review the results, and decide what to export or share.

Find and divide moments

AI SHORTS

Review candidate highlights

Analyze a recording and rank suggested moments for review. Selection can use configured transcription and AI services.

SMART EPISODE SPLIT

Keep clips near a minute

Split long match recordings into approximately one-minute parts and prefer detected scene boundaries where possible. Exact duration and cut points depend on the source footage.

SEQUENTIAL CLIPS

Work through a video in order

Create a sequence of clips from a source recording when you want to review the video in sections.

Shape an export

01

Vertical 9:16 layout

Choose framing and background options for a vertical video composition.

02

Titles and subtitles

Enable or disable titles and subtitles, and review generated clip text before sharing.

03

Themes and overlays

Select an available theme and configure supported overlays in the generation settings.

04

Optional TikTok posting

Connect an authorized account, review the caption and privacy options, then explicitly confirm a selected clip's upload using TikTok's Content Posting API. Availability depends on TikTok's review, account, and permission requirements.

Know before you share

Automated clip selection, captions, and subtitles can be imperfect. Review every exported video and all metadata yourself. You are responsible for having permission to use and share your source material.

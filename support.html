Common checks

The app does not open or the page is unavailable

Confirm that shorts-web.bat is still running in its console window, then open http://127.0.0.1:8000. Check the console for an error and verify the project environment and required packages are installed.

Video processing fails

Check that FFmpeg and FFprobe are installed and on PATH, that the input file can be read, and that enough disk space is available. If you contact support, share the error text with file names and private details removed.

TikTok connection or posting fails

Confirm the Developer Portal credentials and redirect URI match the environment, that the account is authorized, and that the required product and scope are available. TikTok can restrict unaudited apps to private posting. Do not share credentials or authorization tokens with support.

Where are project files stored?

Jobs and generated clips are stored on your computer in the application's configured storage folder. See the Privacy Policy for data handling details.

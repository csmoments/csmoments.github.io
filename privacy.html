CS MOMENTS / YOUR DATA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

CS Moments is a desktop application for creating short clips from video recordings. The application is designed to run on your computer; it does not provide a hosted account or cloud storage service. This policy explains what may be processed by the app and when information leaves your device.

In short

Your source media, generated clips, and project data are stored locally by default. Information is sent to third parties only when needed for a provider you configure or an integration you choose to use, such as AI processing or TikTok publishing.

1. Information processed by the app

  • Video and audio files: files you select for processing, and the clips, subtitles, thumbnails, and intermediate files created from them.
  • Text derived from media: transcripts, subtitle text, clip titles, descriptions, and hashtags created or edited in the app.
  • Preferences and configuration: generation settings, theme choices, provider configuration, and local job status.
  • TikTok authorization data: if you connect TikTok, access and refresh tokens are stored in the app's local storage. On Windows, the app encrypts these tokens using Windows Data Protection for the current user.

We do not operate an online CS Moments account system or receive your local projects through a CS Moments server.

2. Where processing happens

The app runs on your computer and saves its files locally. The actual storage location depends on your installation and configuration. Temporary files, cached transcripts, and generated outputs may remain on your device until you remove them.

Some processing can involve another provider depending on your settings. For example, if you configure an external AI service for moment selection or subtitle translation, the text submitted for that operation may be sent to that service. The app can also use local models and tools. Check your selected provider and its privacy terms before use; each provider handles submitted information under its own policies.

3. TikTok integration

TikTok features are optional. If you choose to connect an account, the app redirects you to TikTok to authorize access. If you choose to publish a clip, the video, caption, privacy choice, and requested posting settings are sent to TikTok using its Content Posting API. TikTok then processes that information under its own terms and privacy policy.

You can disconnect the account in the app. Removing the app's local token file also removes the locally stored authorization data, but it does not delete content already sent to TikTok. Use TikTok's own account controls to manage published content or revoke access.

4. Website and server logs

These informational pages are static and do not include a CS Moments login, contact form, advertising, or analytics scripts. If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical request data under its own policies as part of delivering the site. We do not control or receive GitHub's infrastructure logs.

5. Retention and deletion

Local media, outputs, caches, settings, and authorization files remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall/remove the relevant files. You control local retention. Information submitted to an AI service or TikTok is subject to that provider's retention practices and account controls.

6. Children's privacy

CS Moments is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an online service. The app itself does not provide an online account service.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or its integrations change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date. For privacy questions, contact danil.romoshenko@gmail.com.

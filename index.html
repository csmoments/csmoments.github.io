FROM FULL MATCH TO FAVOURITE PLAY

Your match. Your moments.

CS Moments is made to help with the repetitive parts of clipping. You choose the source and settings, then review what it creates.

01 / SOURCE
↗

Start with a recording

Select a video file from your computer and choose a clip-generation mode.

02 / FIND
⌁

Find moments to review

Use AI-selected highlights, sequential clips, or Smart Episode Split. Results depend on the recording and configured providers.

03 / MAKE
▣

Shape and export

Create vertical clips with optional subtitles, titles, themes, and configurable framing. Keep or adjust the results before sharing.

THE GOOD STUFF, WITHOUT THE HYPE

Made for the
after-round.

Practical tools for turning match footage into shareable edits. No promise that every highlight will be perfect—just a faster place to start.

A

Pick your clipping style

AI highlight selection, sequential clip splitting, and Smart Episode Split are available modes in the app.

↗
B

Format for vertical video

Render clips in a 9:16 layout with options for crop, background, subtitles, titles, and theme overlays.

↗
C

Keep context in the cut

Smart Episode Split can target roughly one-minute parts and favor scene boundaries. This is a heuristic, not a guarantee of perfect cuts.

↗
D

Share when you're ready

An optional TikTok direct-publishing flow is included. It requires your own developer-app setup, account authorization, and TikTok's applicable permissions and approval.

↗

A DESKTOP APP, NOT A HOSTED EDITOR

Your workflow stays on your computer.

The app runs locally and stores project files on your device. If you configure a third-party AI provider or choose to publish to TikTok, the information needed for that action is sent to that provider. Read the privacy policy for details.
